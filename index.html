(📸 ft. a koala who has since pivoted to eucalyptus research)

Hello! I am a Short-Range Wireless Engineer at Huawei Singapore Research Institute. I was previously a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Edinburgh, under the supervision of Prof. Mahesh K. Marina. I earned my Ph.D. from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, under the guidance of Dr. Lei Yang.

My research interests are centered around the Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT), focusing on areas such as indoor localization, wireless sensing, backscatter communication, and physical-layer identification.
