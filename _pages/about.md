Hello! I am a Short-Range Wireless Engineer at [Huawei Singapore Research Institute](https://www.huawei.com/en/). I was previously a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/), under the supervision of [Prof. Mahesh K. Marina](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/mmarina/). I earned my Ph.D. from [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), under the guidance of [Dr. Lei Yang](https://web.comp.polyu.edu.hk/csyanglei/#/pages/profile/about).

My research interests are centered around the **Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT)**, focusing on areas such as indoor localization, wireless sensing, backscatter communication, and physical-layer identification.
